# MacLinen — OS X Linen & Skeuomorphic Era (10.7–10.9)

MacLinen captures the tactile, fabric-and-leather era championed by Scott Forstall: OS X 10.7 Lion, 10.8 Mountain Lion, and 10.9 Mavericks. Lion and Mountain Lion emphasized physical textures; Mavericks began removing many leather and linen treatments. This skill groups the transition as an optional retro style—most famously dark woven linen, stitched calfskin leather, realistic book bindings, and embossed aluminum.

## Core Philosophy
1. **Physical Materials Everywhere:**
   - **Dark Gray Linen:** Woven cross-hatch fabric pattern used for Mission Control, Notification Center, Launchpad, and app lock screens.
   - **Stitched Leather:** Saddle-stitched brown or crimson leather header bars with white stitch dashed lines (Calendar/iCal, Contacts/Address Book).
   - **Stationery Paper:** Yellow legal pad paper with red margin rule and pale blue ruled horizontal lines (Notes).
2. **Unified Aluminum Toolbars:** Matte bead-blasted aluminum headers with etched inset segment controls and recessed icons.
3. **Typography & Insets:**
   - Text has etched white dropshadows underneath (`.shadow(color: .white.opacity(0.8), radius: 0, x: 0, y: 1)`) on light metal, or dark inner shadows on dark linen.
   - Deep recessed wells (recessed borders with dark gradients).

These notes describe a visual interpretation for modern macOS, not a compatibility implementation of the historical operating system.
