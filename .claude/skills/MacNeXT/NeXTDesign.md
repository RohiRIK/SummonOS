# MacNeXT — NeXTSTEP & OPENSTEP Design System (retro era)

MacNeXT captures the dark, industrial, architectonic computing environment created by Steve Jobs at NeXT: NeXTSTEP 3.3 and OPENSTEP 4.2. NeXTSTEP is the direct genetic ancestor of macOS, Cocoa, Interface Builder, and AppKit. Its aesthetic is characterized by deep sculpted 3D bevels, dark gray `#555555` surfaces, crisp black title bars, and floating 48x48 icon tiles.

## Core Philosophy
1. **Deep Sculpted 3D (Chisel):**
   - Controls appear deeply pressed or raised from solid stone/plastic.
   - Highlights: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
   - Shadows: Pure Black (`#000000`) and Deep Charcoal (`#333333`).
   - Inner bevels: 2px wide with sharp corners (no rounded corners).
2. **The Vertical Application Dock:**
   - 48x48 square icon tiles pinned vertically to the right edge of the display.
   - White 3-dot running indicator badge on the tile corner.
3. **Typography:**
   - Helvetica / Ohlfs / Courier monospaced or bold sans-serif.
   - Crisp black text on 3D raised button faces.
4. **Window Chrome:**
   - Solid black or dark charcoal title bar with white bold text.
   - Miniaturize button (`_`) on the left, Close button (`X`) on the right.

These notes describe a visual interpretation for modern macOS, not a compatibility implementation of the historical operating system.
